Privacy policy

The "rclone" OAuth client is used only by its owner, with the open source rclone tool, to copy files between the owner's own computers and the owner's own Google Drive.

  • No data is collected from anyone else, and the app has no other users.
  • Google Drive data is read and written only on the owner's own computers. It is not sent to, stored by or shared with any third party.
  • Access tokens stay in the local rclone configuration on those computers.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
